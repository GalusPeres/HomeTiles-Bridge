# HomeTiles Bridge v0.9.7b2 (beta)

Vacuum maps on their tile's color instead of black.

## Changes

- **Map background.** A panel can name the color it shows behind a picture. Transparent parts of the picture and the bars of a fitted picture take that color instead of black, so a vacuum map sits on its tile's surface and its rounded corners show.

## Update Notes

Beta: install through HACS with "Show beta versions". Includes everything from v0.9.7b1. Older firmware keeps black as before.
