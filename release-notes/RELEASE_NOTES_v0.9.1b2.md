# HomeTiles Bridge v0.9.1b2 (beta)

Icons of the energy sources in the panel's entity picker.

- The energy sources in the Energy tile's picker show the icon the tile shows: their category's (solar, grid, battery, gas, water; costs the currency). Before, the picker showed the sensor's own icon and the tile another one.
- Everything else from v0.9.1b1 is unchanged.

Install: in HACS enable "Show beta versions" for HomeTiles Bridge, update, restart Home Assistant and reload the page with Ctrl+Shift+R.

Validation: 660 Bridge tests pass. Validation in a real Home Assistant installation is in progress.
